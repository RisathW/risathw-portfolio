Why, What, How, When, Who, So far…

2026-10-08

Why I chose this path, what I do now, and the people I'm building it with

Why

I went to Rahula College in Matara from Grade 1 all the way through my O/Levels. It's the only school I ever knew. I sat the scholarship exam and scored 162, and I sat my O/Levels in 2024.

After my O/Levels, one night, I made a decision: I wanted to do a foundation program. At that time the local universities had been locked down because of COVID, and students were being left about a year and a half behind. I didn't want to lose that time, so I started searching for universities where I could study, and I found Curtin.

What

To apply to Curtin, I first had to complete a foundation program at SCU (SLIIT City Uni). Halfway through it, my O/Level results came out: 7 A's and 3 B's.

In my first year at Curtin I chose Cyber Security as my major. Since then I've studied cloud computing on AWS, database engineering and Unix, and I'm now in my second year, at the end of the first semester. Cyber security is the thread through all of it: how systems are built, how they break, and how to protect them.

How

I started designing in Grade 9. Nobody gave me a course. If I liked something, I searched for it anywhere I could and taught myself. Photography went the same way. It's a hobby, and I shoot with a Sony A7 III.

That habit of learning by doing is how Fortechz was built. It's a company I built together with three friends: Luthmin, Kethnula and Navindu. What I'm studying shows up in what we ship. The POS system I built at Fortechz, for example, stores passwords as salted hashes, enforces roles on the server and keeps a tamper-evident audit trail, because security shouldn't be something you add at the end.

When

  • 2024 Sat my O/Levels at Rahula College, then started the foundation program at SCU and began at Curtin.
  • Jan 2025 Started freelancing as a graphic designer at ArtifeX Designs.
  • Jun 2025 Received the President's Scout Award.
  • Jul 2026 Became a co-founder of Fortechz.

Who

I'm building Fortechz with Luthmin, Kethnula and Navindu, three friends who are now my co-founders.

Before that, school gave me swimming and scouting. Scouting is where I earned the President's Scout Award, the highest achievement a scout can reach in Sri Lanka, and where I took part in the 19th National Jamboree in India, the grand finale of its Diamond Jubilee.

So far…

School in Matara, a foundation at SCU, Cyber Security at Curtin, a company with three friends, and a camera and a design habit that started in Grade 9. It isn't finished, and I'm okay with that. A line from the field I'm studying says it better than I can:

Security is a process, not a product.

— Bruce Schneier, Secrets and Lies: Digital Security in a Networked World (2000)
