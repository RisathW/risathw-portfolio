Featured project · Fortechz

POS System

A complete point of sale system: checkout, stock, reports and staff management. It runs as a Windows desktop app and also works on phones and tablets over the local Wi-Fi, built on the Fortechz POS core.

ReactElectronNode.jsPostgreSQLWindows installer

Visit the POS System ↗

Fast checkout

Cash, card and split payments, held bills, petty cash and receipt printing, with returns and refunds handled in the same flow.

Stock control

GRN (stock in), PRN (return to supplier) and wastage, with stock counts, low-stock alerts and supplier records.

Staff, shifts & pre-orders

Staff accounts with role-based access, shifts with end-of-day Z-reports, and pre-orders with deposits.

Secure by design

Salted scrypt password hashes, server-enforced roles, all-or-nothing sales transactions and a hash-chained, append-only audit trail that makes tampering detectable.

Reports & dashboard

A live dashboard and reports for sales, profit, sales by category, stock usage and wastage, pre-orders and staff performance.

Phones & tablets

Any device on the same Wi-Fi opens the same screens in its browser, with live updates between devices and no install needed.

Process? Yeah... I Have One.

Understanding the Brief

I listen first. I break down who it's for, what it needs to say and where it will live before a single shape is drawn.

Research & Exploration

I study the audience, the competition and references, then sketch several directions to find the one worth building.

Design & Iteration

I design with intent, share early and refine on feedback until the identity feels clear, consistent and memorable.

Delivery & Security
